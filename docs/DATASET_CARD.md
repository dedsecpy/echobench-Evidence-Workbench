# Dataset card — synthetic diagnostic v1

## Purpose and status

The bundled generator produces fictional claim/document fixtures to exercise source grouping, abstention, metrics, cache isolation and reproducible reporting. It is not a curated natural-language benchmark and cannot establish the research method's generalization or a real agent's fact-checking accuracy.

The default configuration generates 12 events, 24 conditions per event, 288 cases and 2,016 predictions when run through seven default methods. Truth alternates by event. Entities and measurements are seeded pseudorandom values. The title, URLs, numerical patterns and attribution phrasing are templated and easily recognizable.

## Labels and construction

Gold truth is specified by the synthetic world, not inferred from source agreement. Gold passage relations and origin families follow generation rules. Supporting claims are repeated exactly, rewritten with or without attribution, translated into simple Spanish/French sentences with attribution, embedded in cycles, or independently remeasured. Two conflicting readings remain in each condition. A source can be wrong even when its textual relation is labelled supports.

These labels were generated by code and have not been adjudicated by human subject experts. A restricted fixture parser extracts the measurement value to predict textual relation without consulting gold. This parser is a pipeline diagnostic, not a scientific baseline.

## Independence and confounds

The 288 cases are not independent trials; they share 12 events. Repeated source conditions and model repetitions must remain grouped in splits and statistical resampling. Standardized URLs, entities, templates, source-count cues and attribution sentences are intentional simplifications. Candidate independence remains unknown in inferred graphs even when gold generation supplied known families.

Source truth is counterbalanced independently of the support pattern, so a counting algorithm cannot demonstrate useful truth accuracy on these fixtures. The relevant mechanical result is whether the score changes when copies of the same origin are added. Hidden paraphrase failure and independent-measurement controls are retained to expose limitations.

## Access and artifacts

Generate a new dataset with `rtk proxy node src/cli.js generate --events 12 --seed 20260930 --output data/synthetic.jsonl`. Generated fictional text is part of this project's local artifacts. Any natural source text acquired later has its own access and redistribution conditions; the synthetic card does not grant rights over that text.

## Real-source pilot

`data/pilot-public/cases.jsonl` locally contains one unannotated documentation claim and two public Common Crawl pages. Collection was tested with robots policy, retrieval timestamps and content hashes. This pilot is a technical check and contains neither an independent-origin gold standard nor measured model outputs. It is excluded from version-control suggestions, along with raw snapshots.
